# savorite

## Scope 

Inspired by a Hackathon project that I worked on called Simmer. 

## The Problem

1. Recipes are scattered - screenshots, saved reels, browser tabs, notes
2. Tracking nutrition is tedious
3. Deciding what to cook with what's already in the kitchen is a chore

This app is meant to help decide for you, from what you already have, so dinner isn't just another thing people have to figure out. 

## Features

### Meal Cards

A recipe box for people who are out of decisions. Cook with the ingredients that are already in your kitchen.

The front shows the title and nutrition stamps. Tap it and it flips to the ingredients and steps on the back. 

- Add cards to the meal prepping tab 
- Show nutrition automatically 
- Lets someone copy a card, tweak it, and pass it on.

### Today

Plan a day, one meal card at a time

- Pick any day of the week — Monday through Sunday
- Three slots to fill: **breakfast**, **lunch**, and **dinner**
- Tap any slot to add a card or swap the one that's there
- Three bars fill as you plan — **calories**, **protein**, and **fiber** — against goals of 2,000 cal / 100g protein / 30g fiber
- Each bar shows the running total, so you can see how far a meal moves you
- One button deals you a hand from whatever's in your pantry

### Deal

- Your pantry is a row of ingredient chips — egg, bread, avocado, rice, spinach, chicken, lentils, onion, lemon, tomato, chickpeas, tortilla
- Tap the ones you actually have
- Cards re-rank by the percentage of their ingredients you already own
- The top four show up as your hand, each with its match percentage stamped on the corner

### Log in

- Log in, or switch to create an account
- Show / hide password toggle
- Live password strength meter
- "Remember me" so you stay logged in
- Validation on every field with clear inline errors
- A welcome screen once you're in
